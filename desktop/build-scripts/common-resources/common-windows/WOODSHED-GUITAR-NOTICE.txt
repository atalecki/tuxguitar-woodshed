Woodshed Guitar: third-party licence notice
===========================================

Woodshed Guitar (vst3/Woodshed Guitar.vst3) is the VST3 instrument TuxGuitar ships: plucked strings played through a
NAM capture. It is built from common/TuxGuitar-vst3/native/plugins/woodshed_guitar in the TuxGuitar sources, whose
native build downloads the following from their official sources, pinned by SHA-256 (native/cmake/nam.cmake):

- NeuralAmpModelerCore v0.6.0 (https://github.com/sdatkinson/NeuralAmpModelerCore,
  commit 0b3d3c97b0859a3a8c92a8628c4dd89a25eb5842), under the MIT License below.
- nlohmann/json 3.12.0 (https://github.com/nlohmann/json), as NeuralAmpModelerCore holds it, under the MIT License
  below.
- Eigen 5.0.1 (https://gitlab.com/libeigen/eigen, commit bc3b39870ecb690a623a3f49149a358b95c5781d), under the Mozilla
  Public License 2.0, whose full text is MPL-2.0.txt next to this notice. Its source code form is available at that
  address and commit; the plugin uses it unmodified.
- The Steinberg VST3 SDK, under its MIT licence (see AUDIO-NOTICE.txt).

The profile Woodshed Guitar plays when none is chosen (profiles/RiffSmith Clean.nam) is "RiffSmith Clean", an A2 Neural
Amp Modeler capture of a clean amp channel (amp and cabinet) trained by Matt Hartman on his own rig, from
https://github.com/hartmamt/riffsmith (https://riffsmith.app), under the MIT License below (also in
profiles/RiffSmith-LICENSE.txt). It is kept in the TuxGuitar sources unmodified and pinned by SHA-256
(common/TuxGuitar-vst3/native/plugins/woodshed_guitar/profiles).
