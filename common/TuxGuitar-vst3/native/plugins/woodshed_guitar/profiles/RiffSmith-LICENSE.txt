RiffSmith Clean.nam: the NAM capture Woodshed Guitar plays when no profile is chosen

"RiffSmith Clean", an A2 Neural Amp Modeler capture of a clean amp channel (amp and cabinet), trained by Matt Hartman on
his own rig. Source: https://github.com/hartmamt/riffsmith (public/nam/models/riffsmith-clean.nam, added in commit
56862ee786, 2026-09-02; https://riffsmith.app), distributed there under the MIT License below (the repository's
LICENSE; its public/nam/models/manifest.json notes the capture as "redistributable, shipped in the repo"). Kept here
unmodified, SHA-256 433f93c73abfc86b1ab46adc8ec7e0eaa46a7286727708880c285c10a1642741.
